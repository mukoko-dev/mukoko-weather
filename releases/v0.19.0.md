title: A bottom bar you can see, and a lighter page
---

The bottom bar now stands out from the page and sits a little higher, and the weather page asks less of your phone.

## Improved

- **A bottom bar with clear edges.** The bar, the Map and Locations buttons, your place buttons and the map's controls now have a thin outline, so you can see where each one ends on a light or a dark page. The bar also sits a little higher, away from the edge of the screen.
- **Roomier buttons in the bar.** The buttons in the bottom bar and on the map have more room inside them and between them, so a thumb lands on the right one.
- **A lighter air quality picture.** The air quality card is now a picture of the map with the air quality drawn on it, instead of a second live map. It uses much less memory, which should help phones with little memory.
- **The full map is lighter on very sharp screens.** On phones with the sharpest screens the map is drawn at a slightly lower sharpness, which saves memory. The map may look a little softer there.

## Fixed

- **The air quality card when the map pictures cannot load.** It now shows the air quality reading with a short note, instead of coloured squares on an empty card.
