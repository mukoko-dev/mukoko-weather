title: A smoother rain map
---

The weather map is quicker to start and calmer to use, especially on a slow connection.

## Improved

- **The map layers start loading with the map.** Rain, temperature, wind and cloud no longer wait for the whole map to finish before they begin to load, so they can appear sooner.
- **No blank flash when you move through the forecast.** Stepping through the rain forecast hour by hour keeps the last picture on screen until the next one is ready.

## Fixed

- **No error message when our rain provider is busy.** When it cannot give us a picture for an hour, the map now shows no rain for that moment instead of an error notice.
