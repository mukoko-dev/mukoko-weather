title: The app opens first, the weather follows
---

The app now opens straight away and fills in the weather as it arrives, and moving between places no longer reloads it.

## New

- **Opens even on a slow connection.** After your first visit the app keeps its frame on your phone, so a repeat visit opens at once and the weather fills in a moment later. If you are offline it shows the last weather saved on your device and tells you that it is not live.

## Improved

- **Moving between places no longer reloads the app.** Tapping a dot, swiping, choosing a saved place or picking a search result now keeps the page where it is and loads only that place's weather. The address changes as you go, so you can still share it, and Back returns you to the place before. A place you looked at a moment ago shows straight away without asking again.
- **Weather loads from your own phone.** Your device asks for the weather itself and shows what it saved last time while it waits. Pages open faster and are much smaller to download.
- **A lighter first load.** The weather screen's heavier parts, such as the charts and the animated sky, now load after the page appears instead of before it.

## Fixed

- **No more false "Live weather data temporarily unavailable".** When our servers were busy the page could show made-up seasonal numbers in place of the real forecast. It now shows a skeleton or what was saved on your device, and a "Try again" button that asks again without reloading the page.
- **Home links no longer reload the app.** The Home link in the breadcrumb and the links back to the site from the Help, Privacy and Terms pages open the app without starting over.
