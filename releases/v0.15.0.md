## Improved

- **One bar at the bottom of the phone.** Your places now sit in the middle of the bottom bar, between Map and Locations, instead of in a second bar above it. My Location stays first and never scrolls away, and the weather gets about 56 px more screen. Swiping between places still works.
- **Weather reports ask their follow-up questions from our own weather service.** The one or two questions after you pick a report type are a little cleaner. If the service is busy or slow, you now get the standard questions for that type instead of none.

## Fixed

- **A forecast, even when our main weather source is busy.** Some places showed "The forecast service is slow" or a seasonal estimate for hours. We now keep your last forecast for a place for a day and show it, clearly marked as older, before giving up. If our main source is refusing us, a second free source (MET Norway) takes over until it recovers.
- **Older forecasts say so.** When you are looking at a forecast we could not refresh, a banner says it is the last one we received, and "Updated" counts from when it was really fetched, not from when you opened the app.
