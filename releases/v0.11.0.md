---

This release makes the app load quickly and stay steady on phones, especially iPhones, and makes places outside Africa work as well as places at home.

## Improved

- **Places anywhere in the world.** Searching for a town or city outside Africa now opens its weather page. Visitors outside Africa see weather for where they are on the home page instead of an empty list, and country names are written out in full instead of as short codes.
- **A lighter page for each place.** The small map on a place's page is now a simple map picture, so the page loads faster and uses less data. Tap it to open the full map.
- **Kinder to older phones and data saver.** The moving sky behind the weather becomes a still picture when your phone asks for less motion, has data saver on or is short on memory, and it pauses while you scroll.
- **The rain radar no longer stalls.** When live radar is briefly unavailable, the map carries on straight away instead of waiting for it, and the "now" radar picture is always from the current hour.
- **A refreshed bottom bar on phones.** Map, My Location and Locations sit along the bottom, with your saved places just above. Swiping between places works as before.

## Fixed

- **No more slow load or black screen.** On some phones, mostly iPhones, pages loaded slowly or went black while the app drew everything a second time. Pages now appear straight away.
- **The top bar and the bottom bar stay in place** while you scroll.
- **Sections appear before you reach them** instead of popping in late as you scroll.
- **Even spacing.** Sections with nothing to show no longer leave double gaps on the page.
- **Map credits appear once** instead of twice.

## Security

- **Fewer outside services.** The app's fonts now come from our own site, so your browser no longer contacts an outside font service on every page.
- **Clearer privacy policy.** When you search for a place by name, the words you type can be sent to our place-search provider to find it. The privacy policy now says so.

## Known issues

- **The live rain radar layer can be empty for part of an hour** when demand is high. Forecasts, rain charts and everything else on the page are not affected.
