title: One lasting address for every city
---

The cities built into the app now each have one lasting address, and every old link to them still works.

## Improved

- **One address for each built-in city.** Harare, Nairobi, Lagos and the other cities that come with the app now live at a single address that names the city and ends in a short code for its exact spot on the map. Opening an old address, a bookmark or a shared link takes you straight to the new one, whichever way the link was written.
- **Your saved places carry over.** The places you saved, your Home place and the names you gave your places move to the new addresses by themselves the first time you open the app. Nothing is lost and nothing needs doing.
- **Your last forecast still opens offline.** A forecast your phone saved before the change is found under the new address too, so a saved place does not start empty.

## Known issues

- **Search results may still show the old address for a while.** It keeps working and takes you to the new one.
- **Places you found by searching or by your location keep their own address.** Only the cities that come with the app move.
