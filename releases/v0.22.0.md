The Map and Locations buttons in the phone's bottom bar are now the same size.

## Improved

- **Map and Locations match.** The two buttons at the ends of the bottom bar used to be as wide as their own words, so Locations looked bigger than Map. Both now share one width, and the word always fits inside its button.
