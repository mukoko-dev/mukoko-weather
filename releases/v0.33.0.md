The weather map now opens on a clear satellite picture, and when a live layer cannot load it says so, instead of showing an empty map.

## Improved

- **The map opens on satellite.** A true-colour picture from space, with clouds and terrain in detail and a seven-day loop. It is imagery, not a forecast: it is taken about once a day, and cloud can hide the ground.
- **A clear note when live layers are busy.** Rain, Temperature, Wind and Cloud come from a data provider with limited capacity. When it cannot serve them, the map shows satellite and tells you: "Live rain radar is busy right now. Showing satellite instead." Tap **Try again** to look once more. Your chosen layer is kept, and it comes back the next time you pick it or reload the page after the provider is available again.
- **Your layer is easy to find.** The layer chooser scrolls to the layer you are on, so Satellite is in view on a phone.

## Known issues

- Live Rain, Temperature, Wind and Cloud layers can be unavailable for a while when the data provider is busy. The map tells you when that happens and shows satellite instead.
