Share, Notifications and Locations now sit in the same place, in the same order, on every page. And on a desktop screen the rain behind the weather is one rain, not two.

## New

- **The same three buttons, top right, on every page.** Share, Notifications and Locations, in the same order, size and spacing wherever you are. Over the sky at the top of a place page they sit clear, so the picture shows through; everywhere else they are solid, so they stay easy to see. On wider screens Map and your account follow in the same group.
- **Notifications on a place page, on your phone.** The bell is now there too, not only on the other pages.
- **Share names what you are sharing.** On a place page it shares that place; on any other page it shares the page you are on.

## Fixed

- **One rain, not two.** On a desktop screen the hero drew a still pattern of rain streaks underneath the moving rain, so the rain looked doubled. The still streaks now go as soon as the moving rain is drawing. Stars on a clear night had the same doubling, and are fixed the same way. If your device or settings keep the sky still (reduced motion, Save-Data or a low-memory device), you still see the still sky.

## Known issues

- Between 640 and 767 pixels wide (a small tablet, or a phone turned sideways) the account icon is not in the top bar yet.
