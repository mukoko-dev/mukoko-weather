title: Wildfires on the weather map
---

The weather map has a new Wildfires option that shows where a satellite has detected fire in the last three days.

## New

- **Wildfires on the map.** Open the weather map and choose Wildfires to see red dots where NASA's satellites detected heat that looks like fire. Step back through the last three days to see whether a fire is still burning, and zoom in as far as the satellite can honestly see.
- **A plain note about what a dot means.** The legend says it in words: a dot is heat a satellite sensed, not a confirmed wildfire. It can be a planned burn, a gas flare or industrial heat. The note stays on screen when you fold the layer panel away.

## Improved

- **Layer buttons stay readable when you tab through them.** On the map, moving through the layer buttons with the keyboard now scrolls the one you land on fully into view, instead of leaving it half hidden under the edge.

## Known issues

- **Detections arrive hours late.** The satellite data is published some hours after it is collected, so a new fire can take a while to appear.
- **Cloud can hide a real fire.** A satellite cannot see through cloud, so a missing dot does not mean there is no fire.
- **You cannot report a wildfire or get a wildfire alert yet.** For now the map only shows what the satellite saw.
