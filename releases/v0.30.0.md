title: Reading text is bigger
---

Reading text is now 17px: paragraphs, lists, buttons and form labels are bigger and easier to read on a small phone.

## Improved

- **Reading text is bigger.** The sentences in cards and sheets, the lists, the names on buttons, tabs and menu rows, the labels beside switches and form fields, and the pages that explain the app were drawn at 16px. They are 17px now, from the smallest phone to a desktop screen. Captions and small print stay at 14px, and the large temperature and place name at the top of a place keep their sizes.
- **Titles in the side cards make room.** When a card title has a note or a link beside it and the line is too narrow, the note or link now moves below the title instead of squeezing the title onto three lines.

## Fixed

- **The report choices fit on narrow phones.** On a phone narrower than 391 pixels the first step of Report Weather cut off the word Thunderstorm. Each choice now puts its icon above its name there, so every word shows in full.
- **Long lists of model names no longer run off the screen.** On the developer and widget pages a long name inside a line of text could be wider than a phone and was cut off. It now breaks onto the next line.
- **Service rows on the status page fit.** When a service name and its status do not fit on one line, the status now drops below the name.
- **Cloud and ceiling figures in the airport report no longer crowd.** They share a row when there is room and wrap to the next row when there is not, so the word Unlimited stays inside its card.
- **The day label in the 7-day forecast has room.** The word Today no longer spills into the space beside it.
- **The weather widget for other websites** shows the condition under the temperature at the same larger size.

## Known issues

- **Text takes more lines.** At the bigger size, paragraphs and card notes wrap a little more, so some pages are slightly longer, most on the narrowest phones.
- **Small badges and tags are bigger too.** Status, tag and count badges share the reading size for now, so a few look larger than they need to.
- **The hint in the Explore search box is cut off on narrow phones.** It was already too long to show in full, and now shows a little less of it.
- **The printed flight briefing and link-preview pictures keep their own sizes.**
