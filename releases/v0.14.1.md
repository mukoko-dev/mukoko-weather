## Fixed

- **The offline page now opens when you are offline.** Opening a place you have not looked at before, with no connection, used to end on "Something went wrong". It now shows the offline page with your last saved weather and a way to try again.
- **A slow forecast tells you what is happening.** On a first visit, when the weather takes longer than a few seconds to arrive, the page now says it is still trying to reach the weather service and offers "Try again" straight away, instead of showing only grey placeholders for more than twenty seconds. If it still cannot load, it says so as before.
