title: A lighter weather page
---

The weather page now asks less of your phone's memory, so it is less likely to slow down or reload on an older or busy phone.

## Improved

- **The Air Quality Map is a picture, not a live map.** It still shows the same smooth colour bands, their outlines and the air quality at your place, with the map's credits underneath. It no longer loads a full interactive map inside the page, which was the single heaviest part of the weather page. In our tests the page used about a fifth less memory with the card in view. The full map you can move and zoom is still one tap away on the Map page.
- **A lighter full-screen map on very sharp screens.** The Map page draws at up to twice the screen's sharpness, which looks the same on a phone and uses much less memory on the sharpest ones.

## Fixed

- **The map pictures tell you when their map could not load.** If the map tiles cannot be fetched, the cards now say so and show the air quality reading on its own, instead of leaving broken images.
