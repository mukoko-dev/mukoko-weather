title: An open-sky weather page, a blended forecast, and weather that loads anywhere
---

This release gives the weather page a calmer open-sky top, blends nine global forecast models into one forecast, makes weather work for every point on the map, and makes the app open faster and more reliably, especially when installed on a phone.

## New

- **Your signed-in devices.** The profile page now lists the devices you are signed in on and lets you sign out the others.
- **Place names in the address.** Typing a place name into the address bar, such as koh-samui, now takes you to that place's weather page instead of a "not found" page.
- **Countries and regions in search.** When you search for a country or a region, it now appears as a result too, after the best matching cities, so you can open weather for the area itself.
- **An open sky at the top.** The first thing you see is now the sky itself, painted for the weather where you are, with the temperature, the day's high and low, a one-line outlook and a Share button. It flows into the page instead of sitting in a box.
- **One menu for everything on your phone.** On a phone, the profile button in the sky opens a single menu with your account, My Weather, Use my location, the weather map, notifications, Explore, History and Aviation, and Sign in or Sign out.
- **Tap a tile for the detail.** Wind, UV, feels like, visibility, rain, humidity, pressure, the sun and the moon each open a sheet with the next 24 hours as a chart and a plain explanation.
- **Averages for your place.** A new Averages sheet shows a year of typical temperature and rainfall for your place, and how the coming week and the days just gone compare.
- **Air quality in plain language.** Tap the air quality card to see where today sits on the scale, what it means for you, and each pollutant against the World Health Organization guideline.
- **Moonrise and the week's UV.** The moon sheet now says when the moon next rises, and the UV sheet shows the peak for each coming day.
- **Advice for what you do, on request.** The long activities section is gone from the page. Tap the Community lane to open the advice for each activity you follow, with a 24 hour trend and tips.
- **A grouped settings page.** Your profile now groups account, preferences, devices and appearance in one tidy list, with light, dark and system previews.

## Improved

- **Weather for every point on Earth.** Open ocean, polar regions and empty desert used to be dead ends. Wherever you point or stand, you now get a weather page with the place named by its coordinates when it has no name.
- **A faster, steadier first load.** Weather, search, places and history now come from our own fast servers, so the first screen appears sooner and is less likely to stall or show up blank.
- **Your last forecast shows instantly.** The app now keeps the last forecast for each place on your device and paints it the moment you open the place, then refreshes it in the background. It says how old the data is, and keeps working when your connection is slow or drops.
- **No more blank screen when you open the installed app on iPhone.** The app no longer waits on slow weather before it draws, and it no longer downloads every part of itself up front.
- **Location permission is remembered per device.** The app remembers whether you allowed location on each device and only asks again when it makes sense. Signing in on a new device carries your choices across.
- **A calmer mobile menu and consistent cards.** The bottom menu on phones now floats clear of the screen edge, every card uses the same thin border, and saved places from older versions of the app keep working.
- **A better forecast from nine global models.** The forecast is now a blend of nine of the world's weather models, weighted for where you are, with the strongest models counting for the most. If too few models answer, the page says plainly that it is showing the best single model instead.
- **History fills its own gaps.** The History page reads our recorded weather first and fills any missing days from the NASA and ERA5 archives, so longer ranges, even years back, no longer come up empty. If a source is slow or unavailable, the page says so and offers a retry instead of showing an empty chart.
- **A loading screen that looks like the page.** While the weather loads, you now see the shape of the page, with the sky, the hours and the cards, instead of a splash screen, so nothing jumps when the real weather arrives.
- **Weather colours you already know.** Temperature runs from cold to hot, rain, wind, air quality, UV and flight categories use the colours weather services use around the world, in light and dark.
- **A clearer footer.** The footer names our sister apps as they are called, and links to the Bundu Foundation and the Bundu standards.

## Fixed

- **Search no longer slows down for everyone when one lookup is slow.** A slow places lookup used to hold up every search. Results now appear without waiting on it.
- **You can see where the keyboard is.** On the sky at the top, the focus ring is now clearly visible in the light theme, and the profile menu opens fully on narrow phones instead of being cut off.
