title: Weather loads anywhere, and the app opens faster
---

This release makes weather work for every point on the map, makes typed place names and searches land where you expect, and makes the app open faster and more reliably, especially when installed on a phone.

## New

- **Your signed-in devices.** The profile page now lists the devices you are signed in on and lets you sign out the others.
- **Place names in the address.** Typing a place name into the address bar, such as koh-samui, now takes you to that place's weather page instead of a "not found" page.
- **Countries and regions in search.** When you search for a country or a region, it now appears as a result too, after the best matching cities, so you can open weather for the area itself.

## Improved

- **Weather for every point on Earth.** Open ocean, polar regions and empty desert used to be dead ends. Wherever you point or stand, you now get a weather page with the place named by its coordinates when it has no name.
- **A faster, steadier first load.** Weather, search, places and history now come from our own fast servers, so the first screen appears sooner and is less likely to stall or show up blank.
- **Your last forecast shows instantly.** The app now keeps the last forecast for each place on your device and paints it the moment you open the place, then refreshes it in the background. It says how old the data is, and keeps working when your connection is slow or drops.
- **No more blank screen when you open the installed app on iPhone.** The app no longer waits on slow weather before it draws, and it no longer downloads every part of itself up front.
- **Location permission is remembered per device.** The app remembers whether you allowed location on each device and only asks again when it makes sense. Signing in on a new device carries your choices across.
- **A calmer mobile menu and consistent cards.** The bottom menu on phones now floats clear of the screen edge, every card uses the same thin border, and saved places from older versions of the app keep working.

## Fixed

- **Search no longer slows down for everyone when one lookup is slow.** A slow places lookup used to hold up every search. Results now appear without waiting on it.
