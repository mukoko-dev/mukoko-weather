title: Sheets you can close and pull down
---

The windows that open over the weather, such as My Weather, the detail sheets and the report wizard, are easier to close with one thumb.

## New

- **A close button at the top of every sheet.** It sits in the top corner, large enough to tap, so you no longer have to find the button at the bottom.
- **Pull a sheet down to close it.** On a phone, drag a sheet down and it follows your finger. Pull it far enough, or flick it down, and it closes; let go sooner and it springs back. It does not start when you are scrolling the sheet's content.

## Improved

- **Sheets follow the corners of your screen.** On a phone the top corners of a sheet are rounded to match the curve of the screen.
- **The install prompt sits properly on the screen.** It no longer presses against the left edge of a phone screen.
