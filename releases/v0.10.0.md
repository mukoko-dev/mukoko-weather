This release brings weather made for farming to every place in the app, plus a new way to move around on your phone and a clearer weather map.

## New

- **A farming page for every place.** Open a place and choose Agriculture to see the next 16 days of rain against the water your soil loses, soil moisture and soil temperature at several depths, the best hours to spray, and how much warmth your crops have built up so far.
- **A farm snapshot on each place's page.** One card tells you whether today is a good day to spray, plant, harvest or irrigate, with rain, soil moisture and frost or heat risk at a glance.
- **Advice for your crops.** Pick the crops and livestock you look after in My Weather, and the farming page shows notes for each one, a planting and harvest calendar, and how the last 30 days compare with a normal year.
- **A farming briefing from Shamwari.** When you are signed in, Shamwari writes a short, plain-language briefing for your place and your crops.

## Improved

- **More local advice.** Weather summaries now take each place's seasons, usual climate, soil and crop calendar into account.
- **A new bottom bar on phones.** A floating bar gives you the map, your saved places and your Locations list in one tap.
- **A clearer weather map.** The panel at the bottom folds away, the legend shows units, and the rain timeline has play, pause and step buttons. Times are shown in the place's own time zone.

## Security

- **Stronger protection in your browser.** The site now tells your browser to apply stricter safety rules, and only our own apps can use the parts of our service that are not public.
- **More careful with your location.** When you look at the weather for an exact spot, our analytics and logs keep only a rough area, never your precise position.
