The saved places in the phone's bottom bar now stay put when you scroll them, and a place we had not seen lately gets a real forecast instead of an old one.

## Fixed

- **The places row moves sideways only.** The row of saved places in the bottom bar could be nudged up and down as well as left and right. It now moves left and right and nothing else.
- **A forecast for places we have not seen lately.** When our forecast service could not give a forecast for a place nobody had opened recently, you could be left with an old copy and the note that the forecast service is slow. Your phone now asks the weather provider itself before it gives up, so you see a current forecast.
