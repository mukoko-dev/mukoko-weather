On a phone, the bottom bar now reads Map, My Location, your places and your account, and the list of locations is one tap away at the top right.

## Improved

- **My Location is beside Map again.** The arrow that takes you to where you are now is the second button from the left in the bottom bar, and your saved places scroll after it.
- **Your account is at the far right of the bottom bar.** The round account button shows your photo or initials when you are signed in and a person icon when you are not. Tap it and the menu opens upward from the bar, lined up with the button, so it never runs off the screen.
- **Locations is at the top right.** The list icon sits next to Share at the top of a place, and next to the bell on pages without a sky, such as Locations and Explore, so your full list is one tap away on every page.
- **Desktop is the same as before.**
