title: Small text is easier to read
---

Captions, labels, notes and credits are now at least 14px everywhere in the app, so they are easier to read on a small phone and in bright sunlight.

## Improved

- **Small text is bigger.** The notes under a figure, section labels, times, credits and the small print in cards and lists were drawn between 8 and 13px. None of them is under 14px now, from the smallest phone to a desktop screen.
- **The wind, pressure and sun cards are clearer.** The compass letters, the wind speed, the ends of the pressure scale and the sunrise and sunset times now sit beside or under their dial in readable type, instead of inside the picture where they shrank on a narrow phone. The wind speed no longer lies on top of the compass needle.
- **Chart labels are bigger.** The times and values along the edges of every chart, and the numbers in a chart's pop-up, are now 14px.
- **The weather widget for other websites** uses the same minimum size for its small text.
- **Map credits** on the small radar maps are set at the same size as the rest of the small text.

## Known issues

- **A few labels now take a second line on the narrowest phones.** With bigger small text, a long label can wrap where it used to fit on one line.
- **The printed flight briefing keeps its print sizes.** The PDF briefing from the aviation planner is made for paper, and its smallest text is still smaller than on screen.
- **Link-preview pictures are unchanged.** The small pictures shown when a link is shared keep their own sizes.
- **Body text is not bigger yet.** Only the small text moved up; the main reading text stays the same size for now.
