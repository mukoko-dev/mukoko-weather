title: The account and My Location swap places
---

On a phone, your account now lives in the bottom bar and My Location is at the top, beside Share.

## Improved

- **Your account is in the bottom bar.** The round account button sits at the start of the bottom bar: your photo or initials when you are signed in, a person icon when you are not. Tap it and the menu opens upward from the bar, so it never runs off the screen.
- **My Location is at the top.** The arrow that takes you to where you are now sits next to Share at the top of a place, and next to the bell on pages without a sky, such as Locations and Explore, so it is one tap away on every page.
- **Desktop is the same as before.**

## Known issues

- **The account icon in the top bar is gone on phones.** The account button in the bottom bar replaces it, so there is one account control on screen, not two.
