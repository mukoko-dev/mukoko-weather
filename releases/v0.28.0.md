You can now report a wildfire from the app, the same way you report flooding.

## New

- **Report a wildfire.** In Report Weather, choose Wildfire, tell us whether you can see smoke, flames or both and roughly how far away it is, then send it. People who open that place see your report in its Community Reports, and anyone who follows the place can see it in their alerts list, marked as a community report that has not been checked. It stays for one to three days, depending on how serious you say it is, and the same limit applies as for other reports: five an hour.
- **Always shown as unverified.** A wildfire report is labelled "Community report, unverified". A forecast cannot tell us whether a fire is burning, so the app never marks a wildfire report as verified or confirmed, however hot, dry or windy it is. Open the weather map and choose Wildfires to see what satellites detected, and read the two side by side.
- **A plain note before you send.** The report screen says that this is not an emergency service and asks you to call your local emergency services first if anyone is in danger.

## Known issues

- **A report is what people nearby said, not a confirmed fire.** It can be wrong, out of date, or about a planned burn. Please do not rely on it for a safety decision.
- **It is not an emergency service.** Sending a report does not call the fire service or anyone else for help. If anyone is in danger, call your local emergency services first.
- **No one is sent a message when you send a report.** Nothing goes to anyone's phone outside the app. People see your report only when they open the place or their alerts list in the app.
