## Improved

- **The forecast now recovers on its own.** If the live forecast cannot be loaded when a page opens, the app keeps trying in the background and says why in plain words: busy right now, you are offline, or the forecast service is slow. It tries again when you come back online or return to the app, the warning disappears as soon as live weather arrives, and a "Try again" button runs it straight away.
- **Pages load lighter, so busy shared networks keep working.** A place's air quality, haze, air quality map, airport report and the like are now fetched once and reused while you scroll or swipe between places, instead of again and again. A school or office with many devices on one connection no longer gets cut off for asking too much.
- **The app always opens on weather.** With nothing else to go on, the home page shows Harare instead of a page for choosing a city, and the bottom bar suggests Harare, Nairobi and Lagos until you save places of your own. Search is still one tap away in My Weather, and every place is on the Locations list.
- **Pick a forecast model and the forecast follows it.** Choosing a model in My Weather now bases the whole forecast on that model, and fills in anything it does not publish from the best single guess. If the model has nothing for your place, the page shows the best single forecast instead.
- **New addresses for places.** If you call our public place lookup, nearest-place or search addresses from your own site or script, they now live at weather-api.mukoko.com, the same fast servers the app itself uses. The old weather.mukoko.com addresses for them are switched off, and the Developers page lists the new ones.
- **Smoother scale bars, and a sensible size on a computer.** The air quality and UV index bars now blend smoothly from the low end to the high end instead of showing hard blocks of colour, and the marker is a small dot the same size on a phone and on a wide screen instead of growing with the card. The pollutant bars on the 24-hour trends page and each day's temperature bar in the 7-day forecast blend the same way, with the same colour for the same temperature on every day.

## Fixed

- **Coming back online no longer reloads the app.** The page used to restart every time your phone or laptop reconnected, losing your place.
- **Places we list always open.** Pages such as Harare no longer show "Location not found" when our place lookup is slow or unavailable, and community places such as Sembawang open too. Older-style links such as london-gb now take you to that place when we can find it.
- **Your saved places and settings sync between your devices without errors.** A device that had never synced before now sets itself up once instead of failing in the background.
- **Air quality compares with yesterday again.** The sentence on the air quality card that says whether the air is better, worse or about the same as at this hour yesterday is back.
