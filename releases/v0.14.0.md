## Improved

- **Pick a forecast model and the forecast follows it.** Choosing a model in My Weather now bases the whole forecast on that model, and fills in anything it does not publish from the best single guess. If the model has nothing for your place, the page shows the best single forecast instead.

## Fixed

- **Air quality compares with yesterday again.** The sentence on the air quality card that says whether the air is better, worse or about the same as at this hour yesterday is back.
