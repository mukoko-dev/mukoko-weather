## Improved

- **Pick a forecast model and the forecast follows it.** Choosing a model in My Weather now bases the whole forecast on that model, and fills in anything it does not publish from the best single guess. If the model has nothing for your place, the page shows the best single forecast instead.
- **New addresses for places.** If you call our public place lookup, nearest-place or search addresses from your own site or script, they now live at weather-api.mukoko.com, the same fast servers the app itself uses. The old weather.mukoko.com addresses for them are switched off, and the Developers page lists the new ones.
- **Smoother scale bars, and a sensible size on a computer.** The air quality and UV index bars now blend smoothly from the low end to the high end instead of showing hard blocks of colour, and the marker is a small dot the same size on a phone and on a wide screen instead of growing with the card. The pollutant bars on the 24-hour trends page and each day's temperature bar in the 7-day forecast blend the same way, with the same colour for the same temperature on every day.

## Fixed

- **Air quality compares with yesterday again.** The sentence on the air quality card that says whether the air is better, worse or about the same as at this hour yesterday is back.
