title: Alerts for the places you follow
---

The bell now opens a real list of alerts for your places, and you choose which kinds you want to see.

## New

- **An alerts list behind the bell.** Tap the bell, or Notifications in the phone menu, to see frost, lightning and flooding advisories for the place you are looking at and the places you saved. Each one says what to expect, when, and when the forecast behind it was last updated, in that place's own time. A small dot on the bell tells you there is something to read.
- **Reports from your community.** If people near one of your places have reported flooding in the last day, it appears in the list, clearly marked as a community report that has not been checked.
- **Report from an alert.** On a flooding alert, or when the list is empty, tap Report flooding to tell your community what you are seeing. The report form opens already on flooding, for that place.
- **Choose your alerts.** In Notification settings you can switch Frost, Lightning, Wildfire and Flooding on or off, and set quiet hours that pause the dot on the bell. All four kinds start on and quiet hours start off. You can open the settings from the alerts list, from My Weather and from your profile.
- **A list that tells you what it checked.** When there is nothing to tell you, the list says how many places it looked at and when their forecasts were last updated, instead of just going quiet.

## Improved

- **Quick changes to your settings are no longer lost.** When two of your preference changes happened almost at the same moment, the second one could be forgotten. Both are now kept.
- **Closing a sheet puts you back where you were.** After you close the notifications sheet, keyboard focus returns to the button you opened it from.

## Known issues

- **Alerts are advisories, not official warnings.** Frost, lightning and flooding alerts are worked out on your phone from the forecast it already holds. They are not warnings from a weather service, and they can arrive late or miss an event. Follow your local authorities for official warnings.
- **Alerts reach you inside the app only.** Push messages to your phone, Telegram and Campfire are not switched on yet. The settings say so, and nothing is sent to you outside the app.
- **You cannot report a wildfire yet.** Wildfire alerts come only from what people report, and reporting a wildfire is not available yet, so you will not see a wildfire alert for now. The Wildfire switch is there for when it is.
- **A place you have not opened lately may not be checked.** Alerts use the forecasts your phone has stored. Open a place and it is included.
