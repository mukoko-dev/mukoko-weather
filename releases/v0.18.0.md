Moving between your saved places is quicker, the map layers look smoother, and the bottom bar is easier to use with a thumb.

## New

- **Weather effects now move on iPhone and iPad.** Rain, storms, snow, fog, wind, clouds and clear skies now move behind the weather on iPhone and iPad too. They stay still when your phone is set to reduce motion or to save data.

## Improved

- **Faster swiping between saved places.** While you read one place, the next ones are made ready, so a swipe shows them straight away.
- **Pages open faster.** The page frame now appears without waiting for the forecast, and the forecast fills in as soon as it arrives.
- **A smoother map.** Satellite rain no longer zooms in further than the satellite can really see. Air quality and forecast layers are drawn as smooth shaded areas instead of large squares.
- **A bottom bar that is easier to use.** The bar is taller, its labels and icons are smaller, and every button can be tapped from the whole height of the bar. The tab you are on is marked with a soft highlight. The buttons on the map line up with the bar.
- **Share and your profile stay in reach.** On a phone they stay at the top of the screen while you scroll.
- **The same corners everywhere.** Buttons, badges, cards and loading placeholders now share one set of corners.

## Fixed

- **A map picture that failed is tried again sooner.** If a weather layer picture could not be loaded, it is no longer kept blank for minutes.

## Known issues

- Temperature, wind and cloud layers on the map can show empty patches at times, because our map picture provider limits how many pictures we can fetch. We are working on a second source.
