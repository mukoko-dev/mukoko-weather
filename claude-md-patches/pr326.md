## 5. Line 2211, replace "Legacy slugs are untouched."

> **Canonical slugs: the shipped seeds are `{name}--{ref}` (#317).** Owner
> decision: the shipped places move to this model. The 264 seed places no
> longer have a canonical legacy slug: `harare` is `harare--ksy4dd1`,
> `nairobi-ke` is `nairobi--kzf0tv5` (`{slugifyName(name)}--{geohash7 of the
> seed's coordinate}`; a verified OSM ref is used only where we hold one, and
> the seed holds none). The pairs are PINNED (append-only: a published URL
> never changes). `src/lib/slug-migration.test.ts` pins the table (one row per
> seed, bijective, equals the derivation, never an `--osm-` ref, a canonical
> slug is never a legacy slug); `workers/scripts/gen-seed-locations.mjs`
> writes the Rust copy `workers/crates/weather-core/data/slug-migration.json`
> (CI `--check`) and `weather_core::slug_migration` re-derives every slug from
> the seed. Design note: `docs/canonical-slugs.md`. **Only a slug in the table
> moves.** A community, platform or Nyuchi place (`foo-ke`, `harare-35c223`)
> keeps its slug: its stored history, reports, country, province and tags are
> found by it, and a `{name}--{geohash7}` spot reaches none of them.
>
> - **Every old seed URL redirects forever, in one hop.** The proxy 308s a
>   legacy seed slug, a bare seed name and either in the wrong case straight to
>   the canonical slug, sub-route and query kept, from a table with no lookup
>   (`LEGACY_TO_CANONICAL`, `SEED_NAME_ALIASES`, whose targets are canonical).
>   Nothing else redirects, whatever a lookup answers. A canonical slug is a
>   fixed point, so nothing loops.
> - **Every surface emits the canonical slug of a shipped place.**
>   `catalogueLocation` / `localPlaceForSlug` (`local-place.ts`),
>   `seedLocationForSlug`, `resolveLocationSlug` (`canonicalSlug`, table only)
>   and `defaultHomeLocation` return a location whose `slug` is canonical, and
>   a canonical seed slug is matched as the seed FIRST (own province, tags,
>   height), never as a bare coordinate. Links built from the seed list use
>   `locationPath()`. Worker answers still carry legacy seed slugs
>   (`/v1/search`, `/v1/geo`, resolve): the app canonicalises them
>   (`useLocationQuickSearch`, `detectUserLocation`, the home page, bare-name).
> - **Saved state migrates once, idempotently, table slugs only**
>   (`migrateSlugPrefs` in `applyToStore`, the device-profile pull, the legacy
>   import). The device weather store (`recallPlace`) finds a copy under either
>   spelling and always returns it under the canonical slug.
> - **Durable data of a seed stays under the LEGACY key.** The Workers accept
>   both spellings (`find_seed`, `resolve_seed`) and key by the legacy one:
>   `community::storage_slug` / `read_keys` (MongoDB reports; a canonical seed
>   slug also reads its `geo:<geohash7>` cell key, never writes it),
>   `history::d1_slug` (D1 `place_daily`) and the MongoDB history key. The
>   places Worker's `find_location` goes through `locations::slug_branch`: seed
>   (either spelling), then spot, then the Nyuchi API and community records; a
>   slug without `--` is never a spot.
> - **Deploy order:** the places, forecast and ai Workers BEFORE Vercel
>   promotes this. An old Worker reads a canonical seed slug as a spot (history
>   clamped to 366 days, no recorded days, spot budgets, archive days written
>   under `geo:` keys that are never read).
